## 2026-09-01 — Fade/haze/blur become per-priority (Jorge's report, effect groups)

Jorge was right: fade and haze were whole-layer, keyed to P0's depth
alone (a sunken P1 never darkened; a sunken P0 tinted its in-zone P1
too), and blur selected per-tier but took the farthest tier's strength.

Fix - EFFECT GROUPS: tiers sharing a depth share one pass; when a
layer's tier depths split and any effect is on, the layer draws once
per depth group, each pass with ITS OWN fade/haze tint and ghost
strength, alpha-hiding the other groups. The masks partition the
tiles - nothing draws twice, color math stays exact. Extra passes only
in 3D with effects on and depths split; equal depths degenerate to the
single pass. The atmosphere refactored into
gpu3dsSetStereoAtmosphereForDepth(depth) (one depth's math) + the
layer wrapper; the ghost tier-on machinery was subsumed by the
partition and deleted. The draw block finally became a lambda
(drawLayerContent) shared by the group loop and the spotlight passes.

Validated on the pillar state (P0=0 in-zone, P1=-4 behind, FADE=8):
the pillar alone darkens, the P0 structures stay bright, the fill
keeps the seam continuous. The stale-object rule held: probe build
after a clean flip needed its own make clean.

## 2026-09-01 — #71 first round: merge-by-result groups + per-call fill math

Jorge's field data cracked the Butoden mystery open:

* The "haze that turns itself on" is the GAME's own flicker transparency
  (alternating layers every frame for the split-screen fog) - not our
  effect. What our side DID waste: with priorities split and any effect
  on, a layer paid one pass per DEPTH even when both depths sat inside
  the focus zone - two full section-heavy passes to apply a no-op tint.
  Groups now merge by the effect's RESULT (tint color + ghost params,
  computed by the new pure gpu3dsComputeAtmosphereForDepth): identical
  results share one pass. Butoden's split-screen case collapses back to
  a single pass.
* The fill width (with its slider read) was computed per TILE ROW inside
  the walkers - dozens of times per layer per frame on raster-heavy
  games, 2D included. Hoisted to once per walker call
  (S9xComputePriorityFillWidth), shared by both walkers.
* Validated in Azahar on the pillar state: the per-priority fade scene
  is pixel-equivalent pre/post merge (4% residual = the game's animated
  lights). Remaining for #71: hardware profiling of MMX3's 3D dips
  (fill quad volume is the suspect) and the Butoden savestates.

## 2026-09-01 — #71 round 2: shared-tint base passes

Jorge's retest: round 1 helped but blur still dropped frames on Butoden
(and his "Old" turned out to be 3DS Mode on the New - blur cannot exist
on a real Old, whose slider zeroes the whole stereo path). The "haze at
blur 0" is the game's own flicker transparency made visible by uneven
frame pacing under 3D load.

The remaining fat: with priorities split and blur on, the group loop
paid one BASE pass per group even when the groups' tints were identical
(blur does not tint) - 4 passes where 3 carry the result. Base passes
now merge by tint (union of the groups' dims) and only the ghost passes
run per ghost-parameter set, masked to their tiers. The draw lambda
decomposed into drawPass + drawGhostPasses primitives on the way.

Validated in Azahar on the pillar state, both branches: blur-only split
(shared base + P1 ghosts) pixel-matches the pre-optimization smear;
fade split (two tints, two bases) stays pixel-equivalent (4% residual =
the game's animated lights). Blur's inherent 3-pass cost remains - if
Butoden still drops frames, the next lever is an optional light blur
(single ghost).

## 2026-09-01 — Blur Quality: Light (issue #71, built ahead of Jorge's retest)

## 2026-09-01 — Numbers: Blur Full vs Light (issue #71), Azahar 2126.0

Jorge asked for numbers. No emulator times the 3DS GPU, so a
PROBE_DRAW_STATS build counts what the GPU is handed: draw calls and
vertices per frame, base vs ghost, averaged over 60 frames. MMX3 pillar
savestate, both eyes, BG1 split (P0=0 in zone, P1=+4 out), forced slider:

| scenario            | draws | vertices | ghost draws/verts |
|---------------------|------:|---------:|------------------:|
| A: 3D, blur off     |    14 |    5,849 |            0 / 0  |
| B: blur 6, Full     |    18 |    9,800 |        4 / 3,952  |
| C: blur 6, Light    |    16 |    7,825 |        2 / 1,976  |

Blur Full adds +4 draws / +3,952 verts (+68% of the frame's vertices)
for ONE split layer; Light adds +2 / +1,976 - exactly half the blur's
increment, ~20% fewer vertices for the whole frame. Each additional
blurred layer adds the same again, which is what a section-heavy split
screen multiplies.

Azahar 2126.0 installed (backup Azahar-2125.1.3.bak.app): its new
"Simulate 3DS GPU timings" (simulate_3ds_gpu_timings=true) turns the
Frame ms readout into a load indicator - A 7.07 ms, B 8.96 ms. Menus are
English now (language-detection fix); launch with
`open -a Azahar <path.3dsx>` (the direct binary pops a warning dialog).
It logs "Unimplemented reinterpretation RGBA8 -> D24S8" for the depth
texture written as a color target - an Azahar limitation, the pattern is
hardware-validated (the window prepass always did it).

## 2026-09-02 — .3d indexado pelo título do header, não pelo filename

Jorge: "escolher o arquivo 3D pelo nome pode ser ruim: o shareable fica
mais difícil" - correto. `stereo3d/<filename>.3d` só casa quando o outro
lado tem a ROM com o MESMO nome de arquivo; o conteúdo (profundidades,
perfis de cena por fingerprint) é da ROM, a chave é que era frágil.

- Chave nova = `Memory.ROMName` (título do header, já com espaços finais
  aparados pelo snes9x) sanitizado por `3dsstereokey.h`: trim, `\/:*?"<>|`,
  bytes de controle e não-ASCII viram `_`; sem letra/dígito → 0 e o
  chamador cai no filename como antes. 6 casos em `tests/test_stereo_key.cpp`.
- Um único resolvedor (`stereo3dGamePath(out, n, dir)`) alimenta load,
  save, Copy From, Backup e Restore; `stereo3dLegacyPath` só existe para
  migração.
- Migração: `stereo3dMigrateLegacy` - se `<título>.3d` não existe e
  `<filename>.3d` existe, copia (o legado fica no lugar) e loga
  `[stereo3d] migrated`. Vale para o `.stereo3d-bak` também. O Copy From
  esconde o legado do jogo atual para não aparecer duplicado.
- CRC32 descartado como chave: exato demais (revisão/hack/dump = outro
  arquivo, e o compartilhar morre de novo). Regiões e revisões de um jogo
  compartilham layout - compartilhar o .3d entre elas é o comportamento
  desejado.

## 2026-09-02 — Blur Auto: entrada imediata + histerese adaptativa (issue #71)

Feedback do Jorge após o teste: mais sensível para entrar no Light; só
voltar ao Full "com certeza"; e a janela poder crescer conforme a
quantidade de trocas. Nova regra em `3dsblurauto.h`:
- Entra no Light no PRIMEIRO frame pulado, no próprio frame (antes:
  2 pulos e só ao fechar a janela de 1 s).
- Volta ao Full após `required` janelas limpas seguidas (base 3 = 3 s);
  qualquer pulo no meio reinicia a contagem.
- `required` DOBRA (teto 60 = 1 min) a cada recaída em menos de 10
  janelas depois de voltar ao Full; recaída entre 10 e 30 janelas mantém
  o valor; 30 janelas estáveis em Full devolvem ao base. Um jogo na beira
  assenta no Light em vez de ficar piscando entre os dois.
- 9 casos de teste (entrada imediata, reinício, escalada 3→6→12→24→48→60,
  reset por estabilidade, faixa intermediária).

## 2026-09-02 — Issue #73: update com jogo aberto (malloc) + cursor do Hide Unused

Jorge relatou o erro de alocação ao atualizar com ROM aberta e lembrou que
o fluxo pedido era fechar o jogo e recarregar NA MESMA SESSÃO - o park de

  `[upd] heap before net` via mallinfo.

  ÍNDICE; linhas escondidas acima empurravam o cursor. Agora reencontra a
  linha por (Type, Text) e reposiciona o scroll se ficou fora da janela.
  Vale para toda reconstrução (Copy From, perfis).

1680 B). Desassemblando os 96 bytes antes do PC:

    ldr r2,[r4]            ; dirp->device = 3
    ldr r3,=devoptab_list
    ldr r1,[r3,r2,lsl #2]  ; devoptab_list[3] == NULL
    ldr lr,[r1,#0x44]      ; ->dirnext_r  -> FAR 0x44

É o `readdir` da libsysbase com o dispositivo `sdmc:` já removido. Quem
faz readdir fora da thread principal é `file3dsBgScanThread` (varredura
em segundo plano do navegador, detached); a string `alttp_msu-24.flac`
na pilha é a listagem da pasta do ALTTP MSU que ela percorria. Cadeia:
descarregar o jogo em sessão (#73) → aba Load Game volta → varredura
dispara → usuário sai → `main` retorna → libctru desmonta o sdmc → a
thread cai no próximo readdir. A hipótese anterior (read-ahead do
MSU-1) estava errada; o hardening feito continua válido.
Fix: `bgScanAbort` checado no laço de readdir, `file3dsBgScanShutdown()`
(abort + espera até 3 s) no início de `file3dsFinalize`, e nenhuma
varredura nova depois do abort. Lição: thread detached que toca o SD
precisa de ponto de parada na saída; e o .elf por release (já no CI)
teria poupado a tarde.

## 2026-09-06 — Mode 7: faixa do céu sumia no hardware (fila vs commit)
